# Image credits

All temple images in `apps/mobile/assets/images/temples/` (one per temple, 21 so far) and the
older illustrations in `apps/mobile/assets/images/canva/` were generated in Canva for this
project. They are artistic renderings, not photographs; replace them with the temples' own
photos before launch where possible. Temples without their own image yet fall back to the
`canva/` illustrations.

Partner app service images (`apps/vendor_app/assets/images/services/`) were also generated
in Canva.
